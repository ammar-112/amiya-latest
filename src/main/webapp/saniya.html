Saniya ki tareef

Meri best friend ke liye, jise shukriya kehna kam padta hai.

Saniya, tum woh insaan ho jiske saath chup baithna bhi baat jaisa lagta hai. Tumhari hansi mein itni roshni hai ki andheri raat bhi thodi der ke liye subah lagne lagti hai.

Dil ki saaf

Tumhare dil mein na dikhawa hai na chhal. Jo hai, saaf hai.

Muskurahat

Tumhari muskurahat se room ki battiyan bhi sharma jaati hain.

Samajhdaar

Tum bina bole samajh jaati ho, aur bina jataye sambhal leti ho.

Bharose ki

Tumhare paas rakha raaz tijori se zyada mehfooz hai.

Himmat

Tum girke bhi muskura kar uthne ka hunar jaanti ho.

Meri apni

Tum mera ghar ho, jahan main bina soche laut aata hoon.

Tum mujhe mere sabse bure din mein bhi achha insaan samajhti ho, aur isi wajah se main banna chahta hoon. Tum meri dost nahi, meri sabse badi taqat ho.

“Duniya mein bahut log milenge, par Saniya jaisi dost bas ek baar milti hai.”
