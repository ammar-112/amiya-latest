Friendship Shayari

Kuch alfaaz jo dil se nikle, aur seedhe dost tak pahunche.

Dosti ka matlab

Dosti woh nahi jo roz yaad kare,
dosti woh hai jo bina yaad kiye bhi dil mein rahe.

Tere bina

Duniya mein bheed bahut hai, par tere jaisa koi nahi,
tu hai toh mehfil hai, warna sab kuch sunsaan wahi.

Bharosa

Rishte khoon se nahi, bharose se bante hain,
aur tu woh bharosa hai jo mere har dar mein basta hai.

Raat ki baatein

Chand bhi sun leta hai jo hum raat ko kehte hain,
shayad isliye us raat ki baatein sabse sachchi lagti hain.

Dua

Rab se bas itna maanga hai har dafa,
mera yaar hamesha muskurata rahe, chahe main rota rahun.

Saath

Manzil chahe door ho ya raaste ho tedhe,
jab tak tu saath hai, safar hi manzil lagta hai.

Jhagda

Hum roothte hain, phir hansi se mana lete hain,
yeh dosti hai, yahan naraazgi bhi pyaar jaisi lagti hai.

Umar bhar

Waqt badlega, shehar badlenge, chehre badal jayenge,
par tera mera yeh rishta kabhi purana nahi hoga.
