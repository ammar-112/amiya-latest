Jo shayad kabhi bol nahi paya.

Saniya,

Kabhi kabhi lagta hai ki shukriya kehna bahut chhota lafz hai. Tumne mere har mushkil din ko aasan banaya aur har achhe din ko yaadgar.

Agar kabhi main badal jaun, toh meri purani baatein yaad dila dena. Agar kabhi door chala jaun, toh pukar lena. Main wapas aa jaunga, kyunki ghar wahin hai jahan tum ho.

Hamesha tumhara dost,
Ammar
